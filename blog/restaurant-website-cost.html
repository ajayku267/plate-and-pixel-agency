    What Determines the Cost

    The cost of a restaurant website is determined by a combination of factors:

    • Number of pages — a single-page site is cheaper than a 6-page site
    • Online menu — a proper HTML menu with categories and photos requires more work than a simple PDF link
    • Photo gallery — designing and laying out a gallery takes time
    • Custom design — a fully custom design costs more than adapting a template
    • Online ordering — integrating a real ordering system adds complexity and cost
    • Booking system — reservation integrations add to the price
    • Ongoing hosting and maintenance — whether included or charged separately
    • SEO work — whether basic on-page SEO is included

    Ongoing Costs to Consider

    Beyond the initial build cost, there are a few ongoing costs to be aware of:

    • Domain name — typically ₹700–₹1,500 per year for a .in or .com domain
    • Hosting — typically ₹1,500–₹5,000 per year for reliable hosting. Some agencies include this.
    • Updates and maintenance — minor updates are usually included; major redesigns cost extra

    A good restaurant website typically costs ₹2,000–₹6,500 per year in ongoing hosting and domain costs. That's far less than most businesses spend on paid advertising — and unlike ads, your website continues to work even when you stop paying.

    Is It Worth the Investment?

    For most restaurants and cafés, yes. A well-built website works 24 hours a day to attract customers via Google, display your menu, and convert visitors into customers. Even if a website brings in just two or three additional table bookings per month, it quickly pays for itself.

    Compare ₹3,999 — a one-time payment — to the ongoing monthly cost of Instagram advertising, Zomato commission, or Swiggy fees. In context, a properly built restaurant website is one of the most cost-effective marketing investments available to a small food business.
