Our Story

We Help Food Businesses Look Professional Online.

Plates & Pixels specializes in websites and digital experiences for restaurants, cafés, bars, bakeries, and food businesses.

Why a Food-Focused Agency?

Most web design studios build websites for dentists one day, lawyers the next, and real estate agents the day after. Because they try to serve everyone, their designs remain generic, and they miss the specific details that make a restaurant website actually convert.

We only work with food and hospitality businesses. We know what café and restaurant customers look for when searching on their phones — your menu, your location, your opening hours, your ambiance, and your direct contact channels.

By focusing exclusively on this niche, we build websites that represent your restaurant correctly, look incredibly premium, load instantly, and turn online browsers into real-life paying customers.

Our Design Principles

1. Mobile First, Always Over 80% of restaurant searches happen on mobile devices. Every site we design works flawlessly on mobile screens first.
2. Instant Menu Access Menus should be clean HTML pages that load instantly and are indexable by search engines — not hidden behind blurry PDFs or app logins.
3. Frictionless Conversion We place direct calls-to-action (WhatsApp, reservations, phone calls) exactly where customers expect them.
