Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a restaurant website cost? +

Our pricing starts from Rs 3,999 for a single-page website and goes up to Rs 9,999+ for custom multi-page websites. The final price depends on the features you need. We are happy to discuss your requirements and give you a clear quote.

How long does it take to build my website? +

Simple websites can be completed quickly once we have all the content from you — typically your menu, photos, business name, address, and contact details. The timeline depends on how quickly you can provide the information and how many revisions are needed.

Can you add my menu to the website? +

Yes. All our packages include a menu section. You can share your menu in any format — a PDF, an image, a Google doc, or just a typed list — and we will design it into a clean, readable online menu.

Can you add a WhatsApp button? +

Yes. We add a WhatsApp button to all our websites. When a customer clicks it, it opens a WhatsApp conversation directly with your number. You can use this for orders, reservations, or general inquiries.

Can you connect Google Maps? +

Yes. We embed a Google Maps widget on every website so customers can see exactly where you are and get directions with one tap.

Do I need any technical knowledge? +

No. You do not need to know anything about websites or technology. We handle everything — design, development, and deployment. You just share the content you want on the site and we take care of the rest.

Can you redesign my existing website? +

Yes. If you already have a website but it looks outdated or does not work well on mobile, we can redesign it. We will keep the parts that work and improve what does not.
